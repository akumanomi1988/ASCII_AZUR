# ASCII AZUR v0.37.0 — The Afterlight Pilgrimage

## Complete vertical slice: two playable post-campaign regions

The main trilogy remains intact. After choosing the Crown ending, the east gate of the Crown opens into the **Tide Ossuary**, then the **Garden of the Last Frost** after a consequential coastal verdict. Each is a new deterministic 46×32 world region with unique authored landmarks, NPC, a two-memory exploration route, three visible finite roaming encounters, a permanent player choice, stable biome ecology, an altar and an optional chest.

- **Tide Ossuary** — Edda, the Keeper of Tides, introduces the Shell of Names and the Bone Beacon. Its Well of Returns asks players to anchor the forgotten voices or release them to the sea. Choice and one-time rewards persist.
- **Garden of the Last Frost** — Silas, the Winter Gardener, follows the Glass Seed and Inverted Tree. Three frost witnesses lead to the Vow of Dawn: awaken spring or let the garden rest. The final optional epilogue reflects both decisions.
- **Two new original capturable creatures:** Tideworn (defensive Bruma with life-draining Saltwake) and Frostlark (fast Éter with cleansing Winter Psalm), each with new Canvas2D silhouette and animation rig, integrated signatures, combat, roster, save validation and battle modes.
- **Distinct presentation:** marine shell reliefs, drifting salt spray, frost glyphs, snow-bright crystalline details, new color/contrast palettes, and two procedural scene themes. Both established glyph renderers remain supported.
- **No save schema migration:** old three-, four- and five-region v1 snapshots are accepted and retain every existing terrain tile/landmark; the two new regions are generated once then frozen into the next save. Historical eight-POI Crown snapshots are also preserved.
- **Full integration:** keyboard/mouse/touch landmark actions, quest journal, mission HUD/compass, portal gating, deterministic map, altars, biome encounter and recovery logic, local saves, offline standalone and PWA cache.
- **QA:** dedicated story/choice/one-time reward/200-seed world/persistence/ecology tests; expanded browser acceptance matrix across 7 biomes + battle in both render modes and both backends (32 captures). Real Android hardware and independent player/artist review remain external premium blockers.

W3 becomes eligible for technical closure only after successful main CI, published Pages provenance and public-game smoke; independent W4 traversal/usability testing remains open. This release does not claim premium completion.
