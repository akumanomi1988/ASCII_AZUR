# World and exploration acceptance tasks — v0.53

These are tasks for independent players, not a declaration that human testing happened. Run on the exact deployed source named in `build-info.json`. Record device/browser, controls, prior familiarity, start/end time, completion without hints, observed friction and the locally exported journey report. Never infer physical Android FPS from cloud/browser emulation.

| Task | Player objective | Observable acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start a campaign and inspect the regional journal | Player distinguishes unknown, discovered and resolved sites; no undiscovered name/location leak |
| 2 | Find and rest at the first Lumbre | Rest remains immediate, restores the team and explains the journal/network entry point |
| 3 | Track a known objective, resume the campaign and reach it | Selection persists, directions follow a walkable path, the finite goal retires after completion |
| 4 | Find an authored/secret chest and revisit it | Supplies/relic are useful, finite and shown as resolved; no duplicated resources |
| 5 | Spend exploration Echoes on an existing relic | Player can explain its tradeoff and equip it through the current creature sheet |
| 6 | Complete the Forest's NPC, inscription, chests, rest and witnesses | Journal accurately identifies the remaining real objective, then grants recognition once |
| 7 | Travel between two legitimately visited Lumbres | Player understands source proximity, visited-only destinations and locked story gates; travel grants no supplies/health |
| 8 | Enter, explore and leave the crypt | Warning/principal distinction is understood; companion losses remain permanent; crypt progress and map are stable |
| 9 | Continue after the main finale through both Afterlight chapters | Guidance finds the next chapter, both memories and all witnesses, then keeper epilogues after the permanent choices |
| 10 | Resume after a recognition claim, tracked goal and round trip | No lost objective, changed world, repeated reward or resurrected casualty; exported report remains honest |

Use multiple independent sessions across desktop and the designated physical Android minimum device. Assign the four Afterlight choice combinations across separate campaigns; do not replace earned gameplay with injected prerequisites. For W4 signoff, attach the existing 200-seed automated evidence and observed unaided human mobile navigation. For U4/N4/C4/P4, retain their separate task/session requirements from `AAA_ROADMAP.md`.
