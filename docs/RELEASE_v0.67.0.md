# v0.67.0 — Field hunt ecology and purposeful recruitment

## End-to-end experience
1. In any explored region, choose **Direct Duel / RASTREO DE VÍNCULOS**. The board shows three legitimate regional challenge candidates, their tactical roles/elements, known/bonded research state, counter elements, the best available front-line teammate, finite village lesson progress and inventory capture capacity.
2. Marsh and Spire while their chapters remain open use active Sanctuary transition wild populations rather than revealing and deterministically catching their future fauna; the natural regional species return when their chapters are actually finished. Ossuary/Garden honor the established Afterlight access. This is consistent with `activeWildBiome` and preserves actionable optional duels during progression.
3. Select an encounter to get a combat-derived real-damage and matchup briefing with a consciously selected healthy front-line teammate. Confirming rechecks the current zone, chapter-allowed species and teammate instead of trusting stale modal identifiers.
4. Enter the existing wild combat. Enemy capture, escape, normal EXP and loot, village lesson rewards, saved first sighting, research collection and reserve capacity continue through the same game functions. No alternate reward system.
5. Codex exposes **Prepare Direct Capture** only for a currently available direct challenge recommended by the existing research lead, alongside the Diary route. All other species retain standard natural wild encounters. The new UI remains local/offline.

## Compatibility and safety
- Save-v1 unchanged, no new currency, quest flag, network call or topology. Existing old saves, world snapshots, master campaign arcs and hardware graphics unchanged.
- Invalid, unknown and narrative-future duel species are blocked at confirmation. Combat, dungeon, expedition, evolution/dialog and exhausted party block preparation. Seeing a species does not count as capturing it.
- No silent automatic battles, teleports, purchased capture resources or forced collection.
