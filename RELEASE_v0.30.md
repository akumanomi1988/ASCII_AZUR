- Fifth original, seeded 46×32 world region with an arcanist-violet color language, falling handwritten glyphs, authored music, a navigable road and a unique final boss silhouette/animation rig.
- Oriel, two original memory relics, three finite roaming challenges at levels 13–15, accessible final boss entry with team-lead choice, a charged Ether attack, meaningful choice and an optional post-credits world.

- In-game HUD, mission journal, atlas, story compass, saved NPC/relic flags, challenge IDs and reloads include the finale; saves remain v1 and existing frozen map geometry is preserved across three-/four-/five-zone snapshots.
